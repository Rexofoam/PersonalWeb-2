Intro

I'm a product and project management professional who enjoys turning complex problems into practical digital products that people can actually use.

Over the years, I've worked across startups, government projects, and large enterprise environments, which has given me the opportunity to build products in very different industries and learn new domains quickly. Today, I work on digital transformation and product initiatives at Singapore Airlines, where I collaborate with business teams, engineers, vendors, and users to improve operational processes, explore new technologies, and bring ideas from concept to delivery.

What I enjoy most about product management is the combination of people, technology, and problem-solving. I like understanding why something isn't working, asking the right questions, and figuring out how technology can make the experience simpler, more efficient, or more meaningful.

I'm especially interested in AI, digital transformation, fintech, platform products, and emerging technologies, but more than anything, I'm drawn to opportunities where I can keep learning, build things that matter, and create a real impact.

Outside of work, I'm constantly thinking about what's next - new technology, better ways of working, and how to keep growing both professionally and personally.
